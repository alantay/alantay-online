How it behaves

  • ScrollThe rider pedals along the route to the pass. Wheels and cranks turn with distance travelled, so stopping the scroll stops the bike.
  • PointerThe island tilts a few degrees toward the cursor.
  • IdleThe island floats and clouds drift slowly. Nothing else moves on its own.
  • Reduced motionNo float or drift. The bike jumps to its scroll position without easing.
  • PerformanceRendering pauses when the hero is off screen. In production: lazy-load three.js after first paint, cap pixel ratio at 2, show a still image if WebGL is missing.
